How it works

It's your actual Mac. Just smaller.

Not a copy, not a cloud computer. Duo Mac mirrors your Mac's screen and sends every key, click and swipe straight back to it.

  1. On your Mac A small menu bar app shares your screen and listens for your iPhone. Only the parts that change are sent, so typing costs almost nothing.
  2. A private link Your iPhone connects through your own Tailscale network, end to end encrypted, with a real HTTPS certificate. It never touches the public internet.
  3. On your iPhone The top half shows your Mac, live. The bottom half is a MacBook keyboard and trackpad: click, right-click, scroll, drag, pinch, Mission Control.

Mirrors and controls your Mac

Your apps, your files, your windows. The Mac even gets a display shaped exactly like the Duo's top half, so it fills the screen edge to edge.

Feels like a MacBook

Full keyboard with the function row and ⌘ shortcuts, labelled with your Mac's own layout. Trackpad gestures, key sounds and haptics.

Encrypted, end to end

WireGuard through Tailscale, plus a real HTTPS certificate for your Mac's private address. Only devices on your own network can even reach it.

Unlocked with a passkey

Each iPhone is paired once and approved on the Mac with a matching code. After that it opens with your passkey. No passwords, nothing to steal.

Yours to tune

Text size, quality, scroll direction, where the screen ends and the keyboard begins, sounds, haptics. All with visual settings, right on the iPhone.

Anywhere you are

Café, train, bed, another country. As long as your Mac is awake and online, your Duo reaches it.

Any iPhone

Made for iPhone Duo. Works on any iPhone.

On a regular iPhone you get the same Mac, keyboard and trackpad, stacked on one screen. It works just as well. It's just not as cool.

Install

Up and running in a few minutes

You need a Mac with Apple silicon on macOS 14 or later, and Tailscale (free) on the Mac and the iPhone, signed in to the same account.

  1. Build it Needs the Xcode Command Line Tools (xcode-select --install). 
    git clone https://github.com/craiker/duo-mac.git
cd duo-mac && ./build.sh
  2. Open Duo Mac From Applications or Spotlight. A visual setup guide walks you through Tailscale, HTTPS, Screen Recording and Accessibility, and ticks each step off.
  3. Pair your iPhone Scan the QR code with the Camera, add the page to your Home Screen (it shows the exact taps for Safari or Chrome), open it, and click Allow on the Mac.
